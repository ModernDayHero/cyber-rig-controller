SOUND (VU's motion, or link any pattern to it) runs the motors from the phone's microphone: louder = faster. It levels itself to the room, so it follows the beat, not the volume of the party. SENS: how touchy. The phone asks for the microphone the first time; the mic is only on while SOUND is playing.
SHAKE
Shake the CLUE: the motors give a burst (harder shake, harder burst), then carry on. Needs the CLUE's new firmware. With TEST on, these buttons play it. MATCH LIGHTS for one pattern (it follows what the rig shows until you pick one). RIGHT: motor B the same way as A, mirrored, or still. LEVEL / SPEED scale it; SYNC moves it earlier (−) or later (+) to line it up with the mask by eye. Changes play at once.
